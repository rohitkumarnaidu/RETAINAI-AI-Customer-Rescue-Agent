# Problem Statement — Customer Retention Gap

## Problem Definition
B2B SaaS Customer Success (CS) teams manage dozens to hundreds of accounts per Customer Success Manager (CSM). Because existing tools focus on static lag indicators (e.g. quarterly NPS surveys or renewal dates 30 days away), CSMs discover customer dissatisfaction only after the decision to cancel, disengage, or downgrade has already been made internally by the client.

## Core Pain Points
1. **Siloed Telemetry:** Product usage events, support tickets, NPS/CSAT feedback, and admin login drop-offs live in separate systems (Mixpanel, Zendesk, Salesforce, PostHog), preventing a holistic account view.
2. **Lagging Indicators:** Traditional churn models rely on static point-in-time scores rather than continuous event-driven signal evaluation.
3. **Lack of Root Cause Investigation:** Traditional dashboards show a health drop from 85 to 45 without grounding *why* the drop occurred or connecting it to specific underlying product friction or unanswered tickets.
4. **Actionability Gap:** Standard analytics highlight at-risk accounts without formulating personalized, evidence-grounded retention playbooks.
5. **Open-Loop Disconnect:** When CSMs reach out or take action, outcomes are rarely fed back into a structured memory system to inform future recommendations for similar account profiles.

## Business Impact
* Higher Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) recovery burden.
* Revenue erosion due to preventable Net Retention Rate (NRR) drag.
* CSM fatigue from reactive firefighting rather than proactive rescue interventions.
